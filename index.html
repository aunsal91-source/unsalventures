About

Operators, not only investors.

Unsal Ventures was founded in London by Kadir Ünsal. Before building his own companies he spent seven years in FMCG sales at Mattel and L'Oréal, managing national retail accounts and setting up distributor networks.

Today the group combines that commercial background with its own logistics, packaging and retail operations. We build businesses we run ourselves, and we look for partners who want to grow in the UK.

  • Brands from Türkiye entering the UK and Europe
  • Retail, wholesale and hospitality partnerships
  • Suppliers and landlords for our consumer concepts
